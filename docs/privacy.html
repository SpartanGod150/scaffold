Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Scaffold is a reminder and focus app for Android, Windows and Mac. This page explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your data stays on your own devices.
  • When it travels between your devices, it is end-to-end encrypted. Nobody else can read it, including the developer.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.

What Scaffold stores

Your habits, tasks, projects, notes, calendar events, settings and the names of your devices. All of it is stored on your devices.

Sync between your devices

To keep your devices in sync, Scaffold sends changes through two relay services: Firebase Realtime Database (run by Google) and ntfy.sh. Every message is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) with a key made from your devices' private sync code. The relays only see scrambled data they cannot read. Messages are deleted from the relays within 24 hours.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you sign in with Google, Scaffold asks for access to your calendars so it can:

  • show your events inside Scaffold,
  • add, change and delete the events you edit in Scaffold,
  • create a calendar for Scaffold's own events, if you choose to.

Your Google sign-in key and calendar data stay on your devices. They only move between your devices through the encrypted sync above. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else, and they are not used for ads or to train AI models.

Scaffold's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in Scaffold's settings, or in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Focus locks and permissions

To lock distracting apps and websites, Scaffold can check which app is open and, during a lock, which website your browser shows. This is checked on the device and is not stored or sent anywhere. The list of apps installed on each device is shared with your other devices, encrypted, so you can pick them from any device. Every permission is optional and can be turned off in Scaffold's settings.

Updates

Scaffold checks GitHub for new versions. Like any website, GitHub can see your device's internet address when this happens.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Scaffold from your devices. On Android its data is removed with it. On a computer, also delete Scaffold's data folder: ~/Library/Application Support/Scaffold on Mac, %APPDATA%\Scaffold on Windows. Anything left on the relays disappears within 24 hours. If you connected Google, also remove Scaffold's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Scaffold is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
